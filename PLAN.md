## 2. The "4-Phase" Race-Day Pocket Plan

Forget memorizing 13 separate mile splits. The Berkeley course naturally breaks into **4 distinct phases**. You only need to remember **one target per phase** and **two fueling miles (`Mile 5` & `Mile 9.5`)**:

### The 3 Golden Rules to Remember
1. **Hill Cap (Miles 1–3.5):** Keep HR **`≤173 bpm`** (Power **`<435 W`**) — let your pace float to `7:05–7:15/mi` on the climb.
2. **Downhill Flow (Miles 3.5–7):** Let gravity give you **`6:40–6:48/mi`** for free while HR stays **`~170–173 bpm`**.
3. **Marina Cruise (Miles 7–10.5):** Lock into **`6:50/mi`** (or **`6:45/mi`** for Sub-90 if HR is `≤174 bpm` entering the Marina).

## 3. Pre-Race & Race-Day Nutrition (`78.0 kg`)

### Day Before: Saturday, Nov 14
* **Macro Target:** **470–550 g carbohydrates** (`6–7 g/kg`), low fiber, low fat, moderate lean protein. Front-load calories at breakfast and lunch so dinner stays light.
* **Breakfast (8:30 AM):** Large bowl of oatmeal or 2 bagels with honey/jam + 1 banana + eggs (`~110–130 g carbs`).
* **Lunch (1:00 PM — Biggest Meal):** Large white rice bowl (teriyaki chicken/tofu or poke with white rice) or sourdough sandwich + pretzels + sports drink (`~150–170 g carbs`).
* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Pretzels, rice cakes with honey, or stroopwafel + 500 ml electrolyte drink (`~60–80 g carbs`).
* **Dinner (6:00 PM — Early & Plain):** Plain pasta with light marinara/olive oil or white rice with lean chicken/fish/tofu and white bread (`~120–140 g carbs`).
* **Foods to Avoid:** Broccoli/kale/cruciferous veggies, beans/lentils, spicy/fried food, heavy cream sauces, alcohol.
* **Hydration:** Water + **1–2 electrolyte packets (`~500–800 mg sodium`)** across the day; stop heavy drinking by 8:00 PM for uninterrupted sleep.

### Race Morning: Sunday, Nov 15 (7:30 AM Start)
* **5:00 – 5:30 AM (Breakfast, 2–2.5 hrs pre-race):**
  * **Target:** **`115–150 g` simple carbs** (`1.5–2.0 g/kg`) + **`400–500 ml` electrolyte drink (`~400–500 mg sodium`)** + coffee.
  * **Menu:** 1 plain white bagel (or 2 slices sourdough toast) with honey/jam + 1 ripe banana + 1 bottle of Maurten Drink Mix 160 / Skratch / Gatorade.
* **6:30 AM (60 min pre-race):** Cut off large fluid intake; small sips only.
* **7:00 AM (Warmup):** 8–10 min easy jog (`~9:15/mi`, `<140 bpm`) + 3 × 15s strides at `6:50/mi`.
* **7:15 AM (Starting Corral):** **Pre-race Gel (`25–30 g carbs`)** + 2–3 sips of water.
* **In-Race Fueling:**
  * **Mile 4.5–5.5 (~32–38 min):** **Gel #1 (`25–30 g carbs`)** on the Hopkins/Gilman descent + water at the aid station.
  * **Mile 9.0–9.5 (~62–66 min):** **Gel #2 (`25–30 g carbs`, `50–100 mg caffeine`)** on Frontage Rd with water to power the Mile 11–13 climb.
